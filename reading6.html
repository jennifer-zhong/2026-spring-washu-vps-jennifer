I found the idea of dividing the age of technology into different phases/eras to be really interesting since it lets you see the transition between the mainframe era where computers were very scarce to now almost everyone having a personal computer. I think the idea of ubiquitous computing is interesting because computers, phones, and the internet have become so normalized and ingrained in our society now that I almost didn’t realize it. I really liked the comparison to writing and electricity becoming so commonplace because it made me realize that computers have become so impactful in our everyday lives. Calm technology is a new concept for me but I found it to be interesting. Technology does kind of seem like the opposite of calm but I like the idea of technology making us feel comfortable, at home, and attuned into what is happening around us.
