What is a human-made creation that brings out the best in you? Why? It could be an object, tool, work of art, process, system, environment… or something else human-made. What positive qualities does this creation call forth in you? What positive state(s) does it awaken in you? How does it empower you to express your value and strengths? Why does it have this effect on you? Rather than focusing on tools that augment your productivity or performance on instrumental tasks, pay attention to how human-made things uplift you and allow you to be the best version of yourself.

A human-made creation that brings out the best in me is Google Calendar. This brings out positive qualities in me because it allows me to be more productive, but as a planner, it brings me joy to see everything going on in my life laid out in an organized weekly calendar. Even though it’s just a tool to help me plan out my busy schedule, I feel like it allows me to be the best version of myself. There’s something about color-coding all of my classes and meetings that brings me happiness. I also think adding my travel plans, dinners with friends, and planned facetimes with my family allows me to have things to look forward to. Knowing all of my plans and seeing it all in one place brings a sort of sense of normalcy and routine, but at the same time, it allows me to stay organized and find the joy in the things I have going on in my life. I think without being able to plan and remember everything going on, I would be infinitely more stressed. It makes me more present and attentive to what’s going into my life and minimizes distraction.
