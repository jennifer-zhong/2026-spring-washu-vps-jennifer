Super Normal

Naoto Fukasawa & Jasper Morrison

ABOUT INDEX OBJECTS PHOTOS INTERVIEW

Super Normal is a design movement that celebrates the quiet, unnoticed objects of everyday life. Championed by designers Naoto Fukasawa and Jasper Morrison, Super Normal emphasizes functionality, simplicity, and humility in design - finding beauty in the ordinary and overlooked. Super Normal objects are often mass-produced, unbranded, and designed to blend seamlessly into our daily routines. They prioritize usability and comfort over flashy aesthetics, embodying a sense of familiarity and nostalgia. The movement encourages us to appreciate the subtle details and craftsmanship that go into creating these seemingly mundane items.
