I found this essay really interesting being it made me think about what a website really is. We use websites everyday, but I never really thought about how a website can be anything – it can be a place for your to grow ideas, be creative, or represent yourself in a new way. It’s really amazing how a bunch of files stored on a computer can be something so creative.I really liked the house next to a flowing river metaphor where the interviews were the house’s “flowing water” of nutrients that could sometimes change the website.

If I had a website, I think my website would be my room. My room is a collection of all the things that represent who I am including photos of my best friends and family, posters, a comfy bed, and random things I’ve collected throughout the years. I feel like if I had a website, it would be a collection of things that represent me that build up over time.

If you make a website yourself, you can be more creative. If someone else makes your website, it might look great but it might lack the nuance that allows the website to tell your story and who you are. By making your own website, you can implement design choices and elements that are uniquely you.
