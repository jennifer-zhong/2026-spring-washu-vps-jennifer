What is something you appreciate or long for from the past? Why?
It could be something you lived through, or something from the distant past. It might be a tradition or an antiquated value that you appreciate, an old way of doing things that you prefer / are fond of, or even a personal time or cultural moment that you hope to reconnect with. What do you appreciate about it? What wisdom does it contain? What can it teach us about living well today?

Something I long for from the past is the tradition of celebrating my birthday with my family. Every year we used to have ice cream cake, a big home cooked dinner, and eat “longevity noodles” which are a birthday tradition in both my family and Chinese culture which symbolizes living a long life, My birthday always feels a bit different now that I’m away from home, and while I still get to celebrate with friends, I no longer have this tradition with my family since I’m at school. I appreciate this tradition because it allowed me time to connect with my family and reflect on my year. I can look back at pictures and see myself grow as a person each year ans see the genuine joy on my face. I think now, I find other times to connect with my family especially when I do get to see them in person, and I've found other meaningful ways to celebrate my birthday. I think this can teach us that living well today still requires a deep sense of connection with our loved ones and joyous experiences. Family is extremely important to me and sometimes it’s hard to be away from family for a long period of time, but I think this makes me more appreciative of the time I do get to spend with my family in person.
