What is a ritual, practice, or routine in your life that is important for your psychological wellbeing and/or fulfillment? Why? How does this ritual or behavior contribute to your life? What aspect of it makes it valuable to you? What can it teach us about the nature or ingredients of human fulfillment more broadly?

Something I do every Tuesday at 5pm is FaceTime my best friend from home. We don’t get to see each other very often, but it’s a great way for us to catch each other up on what’s going on in our lives and just talk and laugh about pretty much anything. I haven’t seen her in-person since December 2024, but it almost doesn't feel that way because of this routine. I think doing this is important for my psychological wellbeing because it not only gives me the chance to connect with someone extremely important to me that I don’t get to see often because we live in different states, but it also lets me reflect on my week and share important things going on in my life. I think connection and having someone you can talk to is an important part of human fulfillment. Having stable relationships and friendships makes us feel fulfilled and can make our lives more meaningful. By having this routine of FaceTiming my friend every week I feel a sense of connection to her even if we are hundreds of miles apart. I think a lot of the time friendships drift apart especially after high school or moving away, but I’m glad I have this sense of familiarity every week that I can come back to. I know she’s always there to listen to whatever’s going on in my life and I’ll always be there for her.
