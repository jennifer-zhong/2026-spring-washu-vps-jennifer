I thought this documentary was super interesting. I really appreciate all of the visual effects, videos/images, and colors in the transitions and the use of music throughout. It felt like I was not only watching a documentary but experiencing a piece of art as well. I hadn’t heard of Eno before this, and I found it interesting that even though he is successful, he considers himself to be “musically naive” in the technical sense and instead relies on his imagination and creativity when making music. The music he makes is so unique. I really liked how he said when we are creating something, we are imagining, which is a way of entering another world. I also like how he connects his music and art to nature, especially evolution - starting simple and evolving into more complex ideas. I think this is really insightful and something I can bring into my own art and design. It’s also so cool that his music (and this documentary) changes slightly each time it plays. This is so interesting and an idea I’ve never thought of before, but I love how each time you listen, it can be a totally new experience.
